# Vendor Assignment SOP — Rent-Up / Turn Repairs

GPM's current standard: on unit turns, certain repair categories always go to outside vendors regardless of severity. Everything else defaults to GPM's in-house maintenance staff. This rule set is what PropInspecAI reports must apply to classify every finding, so a reviewer (Jessica, Courtney) can see at a glance who's doing what without re-deriving it by hand.

## Outside Vendor (always, on rent-ups)

- Painting
- Flooring repair or replacement
- Tub tile work or surround replacement
- Sewer backups
- Carpet
- Large drywall work (over one full sheet per area)
- Electrical repairs requiring rewiring or breaker box work
- All HVAC work
- Fence replacement
- Roofing
- Structural work
- Foundation repair
- Tree removal
- Landscaping

## GPM Maintenance Staff (default)

Everything else — minor carpentry and hardware (door/window/blind adjustment or replacement, latches, hinges), small drywall patches (under one full sheet, no full repaint required), minor electrical (outlets, switches, cover plates, fixtures — not rewiring or breaker box), minor plumbing (not sewer backups), caulking, cleaning, general punch-list items.

## Classification notes

- **Painting is vendor-scope as a whole category**, not just "large jobs." If a finding's recommended action includes repainting a surface (even after a GPM-scope patch), the overall task routes to the vendor doing the paint work — don't split a patch-then-paint job into two assignees.
- **Drywall is size-gated**: under one full sheet per area with no accompanying repaint stays with GPM; over one sheet, or any repaint requirement, goes to the vendor.
- **Electrical is scope-gated**: securing/cleaning outlets, switches, and cover plates is GPM; rewiring or breaker box work is vendor.
- When a finding's trade category is ambiguous or spans both (e.g. a wall needs a GPM-scope patch but the room also needs a vendor repaint anyway), default to the vendor if any part of the combined job touches a vendor-scope category.
- This SOP is GPM's own current policy, not a universal industry rule — if it changes, update this file; downstream reports should never hardcode these categories anywhere else.
